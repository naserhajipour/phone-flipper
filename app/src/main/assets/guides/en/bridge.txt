# Radio bridge

Phones cannot transmit at 315, 433, 868 or 915 MHz. A bridge does the radio work while the phone stays the controller and the screen.

## Hardware
- ESP32 development board
- CC1101 module for the target band
- 3.3 V supply, shared ground, short SPI wires
- Optional IR LED plus transistor driver for infrared work

## Wiring concept
- ESP32 SPI to CC1101 SPI
- GDO0 / GDO2 to GPIO inputs for packet detection
- Phone to ESP32 over Bluetooth Classic SPP or BLE UART

## Command protocol
The app sends one ASCII line per command and the firmware answers with a single line starting with OK, ACK or ERR.

- `STATUS` – firmware and radio state
- `SET_FREQUENCY 433920000` – centre frequency in Hz
- `SEND_RAW A1B2C3D4` – raw payload in hexadecimal
- `RECORD_IR_START` – arm the IR receiver
- `PLAY_IR` – replay the captured IR pattern
- `PING` – link check

## Pairing
1. Power the bridge and pair it in the phone Bluetooth settings.
2. Open **Bridge console** and tap the device in the list.
3. Send `STATUS` and confirm an answer appears in the console.

> Transmitting is regulated. Verify the frequency, power and duty cycle allowed in your country before sending anything.
