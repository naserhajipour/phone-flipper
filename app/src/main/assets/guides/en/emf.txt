# Reading magnetic fields

The magnetometer measures total flux density in microtesla (µT). Earth's field alone is roughly 25–65 µT depending on latitude.

## Interpreting the gauge
- **Background** – 0–45 µT, typical indoors away from metal.
- **Elevated** – 45–120 µT, near speakers, motors, transformers or steel frames.
- **Strong** – above 120 µT, close to a magnet, a phone speaker or a mains transformer.

## Method
1. Remove the case and any magnetic mount.
2. Note the baseline in the middle of the room.
3. Approach the object slowly and watch the delta, not the absolute number.
4. Rotate the phone: a static ferrous object changes with orientation, a magnet stays strong.

## What it cannot do
- It does not measure electric fields.
- It cannot tell you whether a field is harmful.
- Phone sensors saturate and are not calibrated instruments.

> A "high" reading usually means the phone is near a speaker magnet, a laptop or a steel beam.
