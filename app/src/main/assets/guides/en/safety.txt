# Safe and legal use

Flipper One is a diagnostic tool. The following rules are not optional.

## Radio
- Transmit only on equipment you own or have written permission to test.
- Never replay signals from car remotes, gates, alarms or access control you do not own.
- Respect national limits: frequency, power and duty cycle.
- Rolling-code and encrypted systems cannot be cloned by a simple replay, and attempting it may be a criminal offence.

## Infrared
- Point the emitter only at devices you control.
- IR is line of sight and harmless at these power levels, but avoid directing it into eyes at close range.

## Optical and audio
- The lens finder uses camera frames only. It never stores images.
- Do not film people without consent; privacy law applies even when you hold the phone.
- The audio lab captures from the microphone; nearby conversations are private.

## Measurement honesty
- EMF values include Earth's field and nearby electronics.
- Reflection detection is a heuristic and produces false positives.
- Ultrasonic readings depend on the microphone response curve.

> If a result would cause you to confront someone, verify it with a second method first.
