# Privacy

Flipper One is built to be verifiable.

## What the app never does
- No INTERNET permission, therefore no uploads, no ads, no telemetry.
- No accounts, no identifiers, no crash reporting.
- No background services.

## What stays on the device
- Preferences and the NFC scan history live in the app's private storage.
- Reports are written to the app cache only when you export them.
- Camera frames and audio buffers exist in memory and are discarded immediately.

## What you control
- **Settings → Local data** clears the NFC history or resets everything.
- **Settings → Language** switches between English and Persian instantly.
- Sharing a report is always an explicit action through the Android share sheet.

## Permissions
Camera, microphone, NFC, Bluetooth and vibration are requested only when the matching tool is opened.
