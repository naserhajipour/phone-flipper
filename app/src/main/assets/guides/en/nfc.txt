# NFC basics

NFC works at 13.56 MHz over a few centimetres. The reader screen shows what a tag really contains.

## What you see
- **UID** – the serial number reported by the tag. It is not secret and can be cloned on many cheap tags.
- **Technology** – NFC-A, NFC-B, NFC-F, NFC-V or Barcode.
- **Payload** – NDEF text, URI, MIME records or an empty tag.

## Practical uses
- Identify unknown tags and cards.
- Read smart-poster URIs before opening them.
- Confirm whether a tag is writable and NDEF-formatted.

## Limits
- Passive reading only: this release does not emulate cards or write tags.
- Encrypted payment cards, passports and secure elements answer with nothing useful.
- Some phones disable the reader when the screen is off.

> Never tap an unknown tag and follow its link blindly: NFC stickers are a known phishing vector.
